# Trademarks and Branding

The ARGUS name, RG Herd name, RG Herd logos, and related project marks are project identity assets.

The software license allows use, modification, and redistribution of the ARGUS code under the terms of the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later. It does not grant permission to use RG Herd branding in a way that causes confusion about source, endorsement, affiliation, or official status.

## Forks and independent deployments

Forks and independent deployments should use their own name and branding unless explicit permission has been granted.

Acceptable examples:

- "Based on ARGUS"
- "Forked from ARGUS"
- "Powered by ARGUS"

Do not present an independent fork or deployment as:

- the official RG Herd ARGUS instance
- operated by RG Herd
- endorsed by RG Herd
- affiliated with RG Herd

## Logos and visual identity

Do not reuse RG Herd or ARGUS logos in a way that suggests official status or affiliation without permission.
